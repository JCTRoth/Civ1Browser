## Diplomacy Module (Civ I–Style)

Implement a simple, turn-based diplomacy system inspired by **Civilization I**.

### Core Features

* **Basic Diplomatic States**
  Civilizations can be in one of several states:

  * Peace
  * Cease-Fire
  * Alliance
  * War

* **Treaties & Agreements**
  Players can negotiate limited agreements:

  * Peace treaties
  * Cease-fire agreements
  * Alliances

  Treaties are fragile and can be broken, with consequences.

* **Diplomat Unit**

  * Diplomat units must physically move to an enemy city or unit to initiate diplomacy.
  * Upon contact, a diplomacy screen opens allowing negotiation.
  * Diplomats can:

    * Propose treaties
    * Demand tribute
    * Attempt bribery
    * Gather basic intelligence

* **Reputation / Attitude System**

  * Each civilization has a simple attitude toward the player (e.g. Friendly, Neutral, Hostile).
  * Breaking treaties, surprise attacks, and repeated aggression worsen reputation.
  * Reputation affects AI willingness to negotiate or trust the player.

* **AI Diplomacy**

  * AI civilizations initiate diplomacy based on:

    * Military strength
    * Proximity
    * Past behavior
  * AI can demand tribute, offer peace, or declare war.

* **Dynamic Outcomes**

  * Alliances can collapse.
  * Peace can turn into war unexpectedly.
  * Diplomacy is intentionally unreliable and risky, reflecting Civ I design.

### UI Requirements

* **Diplomacy Screen**

  * Shows current diplomatic status with each civilization
  * Displays leader portrait and attitude
  * Simple text-based negotiation options

* **Negotiation Interface**

  * Button-based choices (Offer Peace, Demand Tribute, Declare War, etc.)
  * Clear feedback on AI responses
